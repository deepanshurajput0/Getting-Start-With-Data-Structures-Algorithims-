Artificial Intelligence: Transforming the Future

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that can think, learn, and make decisions. Unlike traditional programs that follow strict instructions, AI systems analyze data, identify patterns, and improve performance over time.

AI has its roots in the mid-20th century when scientists like Alan Turing explored the idea of machines imitating human thinking. Today, AI technologies such as machine learning, natural language processing, and computer vision power applications like virtual assistants, self-driving cars, medical diagnosis systems, and recommendation engines.

The benefits of AI are vast. It automates repetitive tasks, increases efficiency, and assists in solving complex problems in healthcare, education, finance, and many other fields.
